Why two are withdrawn

RNG027-10 and RNG029-10 are re-encodings whose axiom sets are missing three and four of RNG029-5's axioms. Lemmas proved from the stronger theory are not theorems of the weaker one, so appending them adds assumptions rather than lemmas and the proofs establish nothing about those problems. Any transfer must assert donor_axioms ⊆ target_axioms or re-verify from scratch.

the lemmas

All six, in full

Written with (x,y,z) for the associator. Each is verified from the alternative-ring axioms before use; the whole 29-node sketch derives in ~19 minutes of wall time.

(x,y,z) = (y,z,x) — the associator is cyclic
(x,y,z) + y(zx) = (yz)x — definition, rearranged
(x,y,z) + (yx)z = y(xz) — definition, rearranged
(xy)x = x(yx) — the flexible law
((xy)z)y = x(y(zy)) — right Moufang
((xy)x)z = x(y(xz)) — left Moufang
method

Decompose, then verify in the scope the edges imply

  1. Draft the route from informal mathematics. The classical one: the associator is trilinear, hence alternating, hence the flexible law, hence Moufang. The first draft had the right mathematics and the wrong shape — it stated Moufang as a step when Moufang was the goal.

  2. Record dependencies, not order. A sketch is a DAG. Each node is verified against the problem's own axioms with its direct parents supplied as axioms — worth ~3000× over proving it standalone.

  3. Treat the budget as a diagnostic. A correct decomposition verifies every node in seconds. A node needing minutes means the sketch is wrong there; the fix is to revise it, not to wait longer.

  4. Read the failures. Three of four faults were mis-wired edges, not hard lemmas, and each was found by comparing against a structurally similar node that worked.

3.3s → timeout

The Teichmüller identity, given five plausible but irrelevant parents. Wrong edges cost more than missing ones — and this one blocked six downstream nodes.

37.9s → 2.1s

A trilinearity lemma, after adding one node the sketch had omitted: the associator definition rearranged additively.

timeout → 1.0s

Left Moufang in associator form, after replacing a single parent copied from its mirror sibling with the correct one.

234 → 4

A donor proof runs 234 lemmas deep; four do the work. Chain length is an artifact of starting from bare axioms, not the structure of the proof.

scope

What does not generalise

The same pipeline, run end to end on three other theories, fails in three distinguishable ways. The verification machinery worked every time — only donor selection failed.

RNG worked because it had three properties at once: identical axiom sets, a deep donor proof, and a donor whose goal was itself a lemma every target needed. Ranking donors by axiom similarity cannot detect the third — two problems can share every axiom and prove unrelated things. That is the next thing to fix, and it is why drafting, not mining, is the primary approach.
